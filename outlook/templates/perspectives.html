These six axes are a starting catalog for US gas research. They are not an AI-selected ranking, historical discoveries or validated drivers. Missing data does not mean an axis is unimportant.

Only completed months are shown. Daily flows use monthly means; weekly stocks and rig counts use the last observation in the month. Changes compare the same month one year earlier, when available. They are not significance tests.

Both observation dates and collection timestamps must be on or before the cutoff. Collection timestamps use the database's recorded calendar date; source publication times and historical revisions are not reconstructed. This is not a validated historical replay.
